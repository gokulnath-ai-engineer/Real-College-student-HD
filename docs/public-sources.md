# Public sources and content boundary

The playable slice uses only public, institution-level information. It does not ship private data, private student records or copied photography.

## Verified public facts used in the slice

CMS College of Engineering's public site states that the institution was established in 2007 in Namakkal district, is co-educational, AICTE-approved and affiliated with Anna University. The same page places the college in Eranapuram, about 8 km from Namakkal and near the Namakkal District Collectorate. It describes a rural, green learning environment and an emphasis on skill development and employment opportunities. [1]

The CMSCE home page publicly lists equipped classrooms, sports, CSE/ECE/EEE labs, a library and transport among campus facilities. It also publishes placement/training activity and public event labels such as Annual Day, Students Induction Program and Women's Day Celebration. These labels are represented as extensible content categories rather than copied event media. [2]

Namakkal is represented through a fictionalized district edge rather than a literal map. Public tourism material describes the town's Anjaneyar temple near the bus stand and hill-related landmarks; only the broad landmark categories are used in the prototype. [3]

## Fictionalized gameplay content

Exact building positions, internal campus layout, traffic density, NPC names, schedules, dialogue, prices, item stats, mission outcomes, company names, interview questions, club rosters and time compression are fictionalized for gameplay. They should not be interpreted as official CMSCE information. The playable map uses public place categories and a stylized, non-photoreal representation.
